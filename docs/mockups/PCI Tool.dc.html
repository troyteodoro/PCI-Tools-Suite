Worth knowing
Your scope confirmation is dated 04 Jun, but the network changed on 21 Jul. Redo it before the assessor arrives — 12.5.2 asks for confirmation after significant change, not just annually.

Tighten the tag manager: a publish that lands on checkout without review is the same finding waiting to recur.
